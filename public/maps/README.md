# India boundary

`india-boundary.geojson` is a simplified WGS84 conversion of the Survey of India's
`Outline_of_India.zip` from its [Outline Maps of India](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/outline-maps-of-india)
page (downloaded 28 October 2026). The source is the Survey of India's official
1:16 million international boundary vector. The polygon was transformed from
the source Lambert Conformal Conic projection and simplified with a 0.008 degree
topology-preserving tolerance for web display.

The Survey of India's [usage notice](https://www.surveyofindia.gov.in/documents/ibd-vectorformat.pdf)
permits website use of the outline and excludes commercial use. Keep the map
attribution visible, and review that notice before reusing the file elsewhere.
